1. About Genie and these terms

Genie — GIDS AI Assistant (“Genie”) is a personal, non-commercial AI assistant project run by an individual owner (“the owner”). It runs on computers the owner controls and is not offered as an online service.

These terms apply to this website and to anyone who uses Genie or signs in to its Microsoft Entra app registration (“you”). The Privacy Statement explains how information is handled.

2. Intended use

Genie is intended to help with personal productivity, technical work, awareness of the computer's state, and automation that you control, including, once it is set up, read-only help with work email.

It is not intended for emergencies, for decisions that need professional judgment without human review, for monitoring other people, or for handling information you are not authorized to access.

3. Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the requests, files, commands, integrations and permissions you choose to give Genie.
  • Review proposed actions before allowing Genie to change files, run commands, access accounts or affect other systems.
  • Use Genie lawfully and in line with your organization's policies, especially for work email and work data.
  • Keep your computer, accounts and credentials secure, and keep backups of important data.
  • Do not put passwords, tokens, private keys or other secrets into prompts, notes or logs.

4. AI-generated output

AI-generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate or out of date. Independently verify important technical, security, financial, legal, medical or operational information, and do not rely on Genie as the only authority for high-impact decisions.

Genie's output is not professional advice. You are responsible for the decisions you make and for anything you send based on Genie's suggestions.

5. Automation and your authorization

  • Genie acts only within the permissions you set for each kind of access: allow, ask or deny. Where a permission is set to “ask”, Genie asks before acting.
  • Administrator-level changes are never automatic. Genie gives you the command to run yourself.
  • Spoken requests are repeated back for confirmation, and risky changes require the keyboard.
  • You can review Genie's activity log, undo many actions, and change or withdraw permissions at any time.

6. Email access

With your sign-in, Genie can read your Microsoft 365 mailbox. It cannot send, change or delete mail, and reply drafts are saved only on the computer and never sent.

Sending replies is planned. It would need an additional Microsoft permission and your explicit approval of each message, and these terms and the Privacy Statement would be updated first. You can sign out and withdraw Genie's access at any time.

7. Third-party software and services

Optional integrations include Microsoft 365, Google's Gemini API, ElevenLabs and Wikipedia. Genie also uses open-source components such as llama.cpp, Ollama, Qwen models, Whisper and Piper.

These services and components are governed by their own terms, licenses and privacy policies. Review them, and the permissions an integration requests, before enabling it.

8. Availability and changes

Genie is a personal project. It may change, become unavailable or be discontinued at any time. Some features are experimental and may need review or manual recovery. There is no guaranteed availability or support response time.

9. Security and acceptable use

Do not use Genie to access accounts, systems or data without authorization; to send spam or to harass, deceive or harm anyone; to break the law; or to bypass its permission checks or safety controls.

Use least-privilege access and keep software up to date. No software can guarantee absolute security. Please report security problems privately, as described in the security policy.
